DVM · MS in Microbiology · Bangladesh Agricultural University

Microbial genomics & antimicrobial resistance

I study antibiotic resistance in bacteria from animals, food, and their environments in Bangladesh.

I am a veterinarian and microbiology researcher. I completed my MS in Microbiology at Bangladesh Agricultural University in October 2026 and work as a Research Assistant in the MTR Lab. My work combines bacterial culture and molecular testing with whole-genome sequence analysis.

I want to understand how resistance genes move between bacterial populations, particularly through plasmids and other mobile genetic elements. I am seeking PhD opportunities in microbial genomics, bacterial evolution, and antimicrobial resistance.
