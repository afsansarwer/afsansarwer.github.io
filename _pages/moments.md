### Sampling horses for my thesis

I collected samples from horses and their surroundings to compare the bacteria we found in each. Back in the lab, I tested how those isolates responded to antibiotics.

### Presenting our seafood study at GAU · 2025

I presented our work on antibiotic-resistant *E. coli* from seafood in Bangladesh at Gazipur Agricultural University. Our team received the Best Poster Presentation award in Fisheries and Blue Economy.
