## Conference presentations

### Genomic Epidemiology and Plasmid-Associated Antimicrobial Resistance of Vegetable-Derived *Escherichia coli* in Bangladesh

**Poster presentation**, BSVER ASCon XXXII, Bangladesh.

This work investigated the genomic epidemiology and antimicrobial resistance characteristics of vegetable-derived *Escherichia coli* isolates from Bangladesh using whole-genome sequencing and comparative genomic analysis. The study included analysis of antimicrobial resistance genes, virulence-associated genes, plasmid replicons, mobile genetic elements, multilocus sequence typing, serotypes, pangenome structure, and plasmid-associated resistance determinants.
