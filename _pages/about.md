I study antibiotic resistance in bacteria from animals and food in Bangladesh. For my master’s research at Bangladesh Agricultural University, I collected samples from horses and their surroundings and tested the bacteria we found for antibiotic resistance.

I also work with bacterial genomes. In our seafood study, I compared *Escherichia coli* from Bangladesh with isolates collected overseas. I’m now studying the plasmids that carry resistance genes in *E. coli* from vegetables in Dhaka.
