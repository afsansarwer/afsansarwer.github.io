I am a **DVM and MSc researcher** working at the intersection of **microbial genomics, antimicrobial resistance (AMR), comparative genomics, and computational biology**.

My research focuses on understanding how bacteria acquire, maintain, and disseminate antimicrobial resistance and other adaptive traits across **human, animal, food, and environmental interfaces**. I use **whole-genome sequencing, comparative genomics, pangenomics, plasmid analysis, and genomic epidemiology** to investigate bacterial evolution and the dissemination of AMR.

My research experience has particularly focused on **_Escherichia coli_**, including comparative genomic analysis of Bangladeshi isolates and publicly available genomes, characterization of antimicrobial resistance and virulence determinants, plasmid profiling, mobile genetic elements, and genomic epidemiology.

I am also interested in using **bioinformatics, statistical approaches, and machine learning/deep learning** to identify biologically meaningful patterns in bacterial genomes and develop computational approaches for understanding and predicting antimicrobial resistance.

More broadly, I am interested in integrating **microbiology, genomics, computational biology, and data science** to study bacterial evolution and address problems in **antimicrobial resistance and One Health**.

### Research interests

- Microbial genomics and bacterial evolution
- Antimicrobial resistance and genomic surveillance
- Whole-genome sequencing and comparative genomics
- Pangenomics and genomic epidemiology
- Plasmid biology and mobile genetic elements
- Bioinformatics and computational microbiology
- Machine learning for microbial genomics and AMR prediction
- One Health

I am currently completing my **MSc in Microbiology at Bangladesh Agricultural University**, following my **Doctor of Veterinary Medicine (DVM)** degree from the same university.
